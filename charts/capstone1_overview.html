The constraint

Duty agents cannot give every Idealista/website enquiry the same speed. HOT buyers and motivated sellers wait behind browsers and spam. Consumer ChatGPT is already in the shadows — uncontrolled and unlogged.

Capacity sketch: 180 weekly enquiries vs 8 agents × 6 quality conversations × 5 days. Buying more portal credits without triage burns seniors.

One inspectable path

1 · Score→ 2 · Explain→ 3 · Draft→ 4 · Approve / reject

Shared engine: src/scoring_engine.py (Pydantic + deterministic rules). POC: n8n. MVP: LangGraph + Streamlit HITL. Nothing auto-sends.

Hard product rules

  • Synthetic lead_* IDs only — no invented live client PII
  • Draft + HITL only — no Gmail/Twilio auto-send
  • No nationality, neighbourhood, or language ability as creditworthiness proxies
  • GDPR / EU AI Act docs are a consultant first-pass — not certification
  • Round 1: no LangSmith project in the submission pack
  • Banned rationale terms audited (nationality, race, gender, religion, …)
