Capstone1 · Lead desk UX

How people use it — and what they gain

One Streamlit path: score → explain → draft → human approve/reject. Nothing is auto-sent. Benefits land for duty agents first, then ops and Chleo.

Primary user

Duty agent

Opens the lead desk when a portal/website enquiry lands. Needs speed without more admin.

  • Paste or pick the enquiry
  • Read band + why + next action
  • Edit draft, then Approve or Reject
  • Sends from their own email/CRM — outside this app

Champion

Ops manager

Owns shadow mode and weekly disagreement review. Cares about logs and a simple runbook.

  • Watches HOT agreement vs seniors
  • Tunes rules when agents override often
  • Keeps banned-attribute audits clean

Buyer of the pilot

Chleo (CEO)

Wants transparency and ROI — not a black-box “robot agent” or a scandal.

  • Sees inspectable HOT/WARM/COLD criteria
  • Pilot KPIs: speed, agreement, zero auto-send
  • Go / no-go after 60 days

Enquirers benefit indirectly: fairer, faster first contact on HOT leads — never an automated rejection.

One enquiry, five steps

1 · Intake
Enquiry arrives

Idealista, website, walk-in. Agent opens Streamlit lead desk (or CSV in pilot).

→
2 · Score
Click “Score this lead”

rules-v1 (or LLM-assisted draft) returns band, score, rationale, next action.

→
3 · Understand
Read the why

Only allowed signals: intent, budget, timing, views, contactability. Guardrail blocks banned language.

→
4 · Draft
Edit the reply

Portuguese template or LLM draft. Agent rewrites freely — model is not the decision.

→
5 · HITL
Approve or Reject

Approve = “ready for me to send”. Reject = park without that draft. App never sends.

Example: “Quero comprar T2 em Lisboa… visitar amanhã” → HOT · 92 → next action “call within 60 minutes” → agent approves draft → copies to email.

Before (today)

  • 180 weekly enquiries; seniors drown in browsers and spam
  • HOT buyers wait while agents clear the queue FIFO
  • Shadow ChatGPT paste — no log, no policy, RGPD risk
  • No shared language for “why we called / didn’t”

After (with lead desk)

  • HOT surfaces first with a plain-language rationale
  • COLD parks in low-frequency drip — no rush call
  • Draft gated by Approve; zero outbound without a person
  • Ops can audit band, version, override — not a black box

What “benefit” means in the pilot

Speed
HOT first-touch median < 60 min in office hours
Trust
Senior-agent agreement on HOT ≥ 90% on a weekly 30-lead sample
Safety
0 messages sent by the app · 0 banned attributes in production rationales
Money
Illustrative: recovering 1 extra instruction / quarter (~€10–15k) already covers the ~€10k base pilot

Duty agent gains

  • Knows who to call now vs park
  • Starts from a draft instead of a blank screen
  • Can override anytime — AI suggests, they decide
  • Less time explaining “why this lead” to managers

Ops & Chleo gain

  • Transparent bands instead of gut feel alone
  • Shadow mode before any process change
  • Lower risk than uncontrolled consumer ChatGPT
  • Clear kill criteria if agreement or ROI fails

Explicit non-benefits (honesty for Chleo)

This app does not auto-book visits, score creditworthiness, value properties, or replace agents. Benefits are triage + inspectable assist — measured in the 60-day pilot, not promised from the synthetic chart.
